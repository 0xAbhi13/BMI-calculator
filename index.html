BMI categories

Standard adult ranges used by this calculator.

  • Underweight BMI below 18.5
  • Normal weight BMI 18.5 to below 25
  • Overweight BMI 25 to below 30
  • Obesity BMI 30 and above

Formula

  • BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m) × height (m)
  • height (m) = height (cm) ÷ 100

Example: 65 kg ÷ (1.70 m × 1.70 m) = 22.49. The result is rounded to two decimal places for display; the category is decided from the full-precision value.
